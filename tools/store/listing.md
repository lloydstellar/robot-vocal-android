# Play Store listing

Google Play matches what people type against the title (weighs most), the
short description and the full description, and ranks by installs, ratings
and how often a visit becomes an install. So the words musicians search for
go in the title and the first lines, once or twice, in normal sentences:
robot voice, vocoder, text to speech, rap, beat, BPM, WAV, Ableton Live.
Words such as "best" or "#1" and repeated keywords break the metadata policy.

Category: **Music & Audio**. Tags in the Play Console: Music production, Audio editor, Music.

## English (default)

**App name** (27 of 30): `Robot Vocal: Text to Speech`

**Short description** (74 of 80):
`Type lyrics, hear robot vocals locked to your BPM. Save WAVs for your DAW.`

**Full description:**

Robot Vocal turns typed lyrics into a robot rapping on your beat. Every syllable starts on a step of the grid, at the tempo you set, so the vocal sits in time without editing.

Type your lines and pick a flow: robot 8ths, one word per beat, triplets, half-time trap, boom bap with swing, rapid 16ths and more. Each flow places the syllables the way rappers do: stressed syllables on the strong steps, rests at commas, the rhyme on beat four. Move any syllable earlier or later on a step grid like a drum machine's.

Pick one of 49 voices from six kinds of speech synthesis made in the app itself: formant voices from clean to 1970s chip, home computer voices that speak the way the software of 1982 did, talking computers of the mid-1980s (some of them sing a tune over your notes), the diphone voice of 1990s speech software, a voice on the speech chip of a 1978 talking spelling toy, and the robot and Klatt voices of eSpeak NG. Then put an effect on it: twenty-band vocoders that sing your chosen note, talkboxes, a speech chip at 8 kHz, ring modulation, combs, phasers, a gate in 16ths. Add a delay on the beat, an echo on the last word of every line, or a plate.

Check the timing against an example beat: electro, Miami bass, boom bap, half-time, house, techno or a click. The beat is for listening only and never ends up in a file.

SAVE WAV writes 48 kHz mono or stereo, 24-bit, 16-bit or 32-bit float, exactly a whole number of bars, with echoes that ring past the end folded back to the start, so it loops without a gap. SEND TO LIVE puts it on a new track in Ableton Live with LA2L, warped to the tempo of your Set.

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app only goes online when you send to Live. Robot Vocal is free software under the GNU GPL 3.0; the source code is on GitHub.

Made by Lloyd Stellar, an electro producer from Hilversum.

Robot Vocal maakt van getypte tekst een robot die op jouw beat rapt. Elke lettergreep begint op een stap van het grid, op het tempo dat je kiest, dus de vocal staat zonder knippen in de maat.

Kies een van 49 stemmen uit zes soorten spraaksynthese die de app zelf maakt: formantstemmen van strak tot jaren-70-chip, homecomputerstemmen die praten zoals de software van 1982, pratende computers uit het midden van de jaren 80 (een paar zingen een melodie over jouw noten), de difoonstem van spraaksoftware uit de jaren 90, een stem op de spraakchip van een pratende spellingcomputer uit 1978 en de robot- en Klatt-stemmen van eSpeak NG. Voeg een delay op de beat toe, een echo op het laatste woord van elke regel, of een plate.

## Data safety

- **Data collected:** yes, only when the user taps SEND TO LIVE.
  - Audio, "Other audio files": the rendered vocal. Optional (the user chooses to send). Purpose: app functionality.
  - Device or other IDs: the pairing token and an installation id that LA2L keeps on the phone and sends with every call. Optional. Purpose: app functionality.
- **Data shared:** no. The files go to the user's own computer through LA2L's server on lloydstellar.nl, run by the developer.
- **Encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS).
- **Deletion:** the server removes the audio after the import in Live and at the latest after 24 hours; the title, sound names and result after 7 days. UNPAIR revokes the token.
- No account, no ads, no analytics.
